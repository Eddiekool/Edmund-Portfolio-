## The Uncomfortable Truth About Most Google Ads Accounts

After auditing hundreds of accounts, we've found the same patterns repeatedly. The average Google Ads account wastes **40–60% of its budget** on irrelevant clicks, poor landing pages, and misaligned bidding strategies.

The good news: this waste is entirely fixable. Here's the framework we use to consistently 3–4× ROAS for new clients within 90 days.

---

## Step 1: The Account Audit

Before touching a single setting, understand what's happening. Pull these reports:

### Search Terms Report (The Most Valuable Report in Google Ads)

This shows you what people actually typed before clicking your ad. Sort by spend, descending.

Look for:
- **Irrelevant terms getting clicks:** Add as negative keywords immediately
- **High-converting terms not in your keyword list:** Add as exact match
- **Brand terms:** Separate into their own campaign if not already

**Typical finding:** 30–40% of spend is on search terms that have zero relevance to your business.

### Quality Score Analysis

Low Quality Scores (below 6) mean you're paying a **penalty of up to 400%** above what well-structured competitors pay for the same click.

Quality Score is determined by:
1. Expected click-through rate
2. Ad relevance
3. Landing page experience

Each of these is fixable.

## Step 2: Campaign Structure Overhaul

The most impactful thing you can do for ROAS is restructure your campaigns.

### The SKAG Alternative: Tightly Themed Ad Groups

Single Keyword Ad Groups (SKAGs) are outdated in the broad match / Smart Bidding era. Instead, use **tightly themed ad groups** where every keyword in the group shares the same intent.

**Example (Wrong):**
- Ad Group: "Running Shoes"
- Keywords: running shoes, trail running shoes, best running shoes for flat feet, kids running shoes

**Example (Right):**
- Ad Group: "Trail Running Shoes"
- Ad Group: "Road Running Shoes"  
- Ad Group: "Kids Running Shoes"

Each ad group gets ads and landing pages specifically written for that intent.

### Campaign Segmentation by Intent

Split campaigns by funnel stage:

1. **Brand campaigns** — your brand name, highest ROAS, should never be turned off
2. **Competitor campaigns** — competitors' brand names, expensive but high intent
3. **Category campaigns** — broad product/service keywords, mid-funnel
4. **Long-tail campaigns** — specific, high-intent phrases, best ROAS potential

## Step 3: Bidding Strategy Selection

Choosing the wrong bidding strategy is one of the most common and costly mistakes.

### The Bidding Hierarchy

**New accounts (< 30 conversions/month):** Start with Manual CPC or Maximise Clicks with a bid cap. Smart Bidding needs data to work — give it without enough conversions and it'll burn your budget.

**Growing accounts (30–100 conversions/month):** Transition to Target CPA. Set your target based on actual data, not wishful thinking.

**Mature accounts (100+ conversions/month):** Target ROAS is optimal. The algorithm has enough data to optimise effectively.

**Common mistake:** Switching to Target ROAS too early and then wondering why performance collapsed. The algorithm needs at least 50 conversions in the past 30 days to work properly.

## Step 4: Ad Copy That Converts

Most ad copy is generic. Generic copy gets generic results.

### The 3C Framework for Ad Copy

**Clear:** Does the searcher immediately understand what you offer?

**Credible:** Do you have proof points? (awards, years in business, client count, certifications)

**Compelling:** Is there a reason to act now? (offer, urgency, unique value)

### Responsive Search Ads Best Practices

- Write 15 unique headlines (don't repeat yourself)
- Include your target keyword in at least 2 headlines
- Call out your #1 differentiator explicitly
- Always use ad extensions: sitelinks (minimum 4), callouts, structured snippets, call extension
- A/B test by pinning headline 1 to your primary message and testing headline 2 and 3

## Step 5: Landing Page Alignment

This is where most ROAS improvements are hiding. The click is only half the battle.

### Message Match Is Non-Negotiable

If your ad says "Get 30% Off Running Shoes Today" and your landing page says "Welcome to Acme Sports" — you've just wasted that click.

The landing page headline should mirror the ad. The offer should be prominent above the fold.

### Landing Page Conversion Checklist

- [ ] Headline matches the ad's main promise
- [ ] Value proposition clear within 3 seconds
- [ ] CTA visible without scrolling (above the fold)
- [ ] Social proof (reviews, client logos, case studies) prominent
- [ ] Page loads in under 2 seconds on mobile
- [ ] Form has fewer than 5 fields
- [ ] Trust signals visible (security badge, money-back guarantee, certifications)

**Typical CRO improvement:** Fixing landing page alignment alone improves conversion rate by 50–150%.

---

## Step 6: Conversion Tracking Accuracy

You cannot optimise what you cannot measure. Broken or incomplete conversion tracking is the hidden killer of ROAS.

### Audit Your Conversion Actions

Go to Tools → Conversions. Check:
- Are all key actions tracked? (form submissions, calls, purchases, chat starts)
- Are any conversions double-counted?
- Are micro-conversions weighted appropriately vs. macro-conversions?
- Is Enhanced Conversions enabled?

### Offline Conversion Import

For B2B, where the actual sale happens in a call or meeting — not online — import offline conversion data back into Google Ads. This allows Smart Bidding to optimise for qualified leads, not just form fills.

**Days 61–90:** Scale winning campaigns, test new audiences and match types.
*Expected result: Full ROAS improvement of 2–4× baseline.*
